Computational neuroscience · In progress

Discriminating Theories of Visual Cortex by Their Prohibitions

Sufficiency is not actuality: a model that fits the data has not shown it is the brain's mechanism.

A method that compares theories by what they forbid, not what they predict, with a five-level scale of evidence. It is being built on three models of early vision to find where they diverge.

Psychophysiology · RDoC · Planned · Jan 2027

Detecting Gradual, Coordinated Physiological Drift

Can we catch slow change after trauma before any single measure looks abnormal?

Each person is compared with their own predicted trajectory. A Kalman filter measures daily surprise, splits it into coordinated and decoupling parts, and accumulates the evidence over time. The study is a pre-registered simulation.
