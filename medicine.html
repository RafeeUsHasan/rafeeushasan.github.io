Medicine & Service

Before neuroscience

Six years as a medical officer in the Bangladesh Army: in hospital wards, in the hills of Bandarban, and on the front line of a pandemic. Those years shape the questions I ask now.

The clinic

Worked with the psychiatry department at Combined Military Hospital, Chattogram, studying cases and following patients over time. Six years of emergency, outpatient and disaster medicine.

Medicine under stress

Front-line care during COVID-19 and field medicine across 112 operations, including evacuations and the 46-day care and repatriation of 288 security personnel, with treatment of 9 gunshot wounds.

Building systems

Set up a 10-bed field facility and laboratory serving about 1,000 people. Co-designed a system that let two officers run a 1,000-soldier battalion. Collected a clinical dataset under field conditions.

Turning to theory

Since 2026 I have worked as an independent researcher on formal model comparison and a pre-registered simulation study. Two papers are in preparation.
