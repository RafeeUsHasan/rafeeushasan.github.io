2024

Border Guard Bangladesh Medal

The highest award of Border Guard Bangladesh for gallantry and distinguished service. I am the only medical officer in the country's history to receive it, and the youngest officer among its recipients.

Awarded by the Director General, Border Guard Bangladesh, for medical campaigns, anti-malaria programs and special operations in the Bandarban hills.
